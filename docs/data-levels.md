# Data levels (approved)

The three levels are approved. Participant names may leave the office network,
and appear in a product, only with the Boss's permission for that item.

| Level | Examples | Claude Team | Telegram message text | Free consumer apps (ChatGPT Free, Gemini app) | Local model (gpt-oss) | Names in a final product |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 Public | Published news, journals, regulations | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| 2 Internal | Field notes without names, draft briefs, plans | Yes | Short summary and Notion link | No | Yes | n/a |
| 3 Restricted | Participant names, intelligence, unpublished assessments | Yes | Notion link only | No | Yes | Only with Boss permission |

## In practice

- Everything the relay saves starts at **3 Restricted**. Claude may mark clearly
  public, published material as 1 Public. Nobody lowers a level silently.
- **Name permission** decides whether names appear in a product: `Not asked`
  and `Use codes` mean codes or initials only; `Allowed` means names are fine.
  The bot asks **Allow names / Use codes** before a draft that would name people.
- Do not forward Level 3 documents to the bot. Upload them to Notion instead.
- Consumer apps get Level 1 only. If the Gemini app is used for research, turn
  off Gemini Apps Activity first.
- The Agent Log holds task titles and one-line status, never content.
- Review these levels with the information-security or legal unit every quarter.
